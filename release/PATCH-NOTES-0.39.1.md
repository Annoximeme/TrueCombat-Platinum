# TrueCombat: Platinum 0.39.1
A fix update for 0.39.0: working voice chat, steadier shadows, cleaner competitive matches and muzzle flashes that sit where they should.

## Graphics
- Brightness now defaults to 1.0 for a darker, more defined image (your own brightness setting is kept).
- Fixed sky flicker around the sun on Ultra.
- Your shadow no longer stays on screen after you die or while spectating a teammate.
- Fixed: flickering shadows on grass and fences (DX12).
- Fixed: player shadows sometimes started away from the player (DX12).
- Fixed: a rare one-frame flicker when a frame needed a lot of geometry (DX12).
- Crash reports now include more detail to help us fix graphics driver crashes.

## Gameplay
- The M4 SOPMOD's red dot no longer hangs in the air when you push the gun against a wall.
- Running out of breath while holding sprint no longer makes your view stutter.
- Standing, crouching and going prone now look smoother, in first and third person.
- You can no longer fire while your weapon is pulled back against a wall.
- Small steps and thresholds under low ceilings and door frames no longer stop you.
- Muzzle flashes now sit at the end of the barrel and follow the gun on every weapon, in first and third person, including both guns when firing akimbo.
```

```
## Voice chat
- Fixed voice activation never transmitting on real microphones, and the push-to-talk keys now always transmit.
- Options > Voice now lets you pick your microphone and output device, with a live microphone level meter.

## Matches and interface
- Voice and callout icons over heads now only show over your own teammates, so they no longer give away an enemy's position through walls. Servers can turn them off, and players can hide them with cg_voiceSprites 0.
- Changing team now tells you on screen when the switch happens, or why it was refused.
- Competitive matches: round numbers, round results and the scoreboard now follow the match, and side swaps no longer flood the feed or put the whole team on one spawn spot.
- Community map names now read properly on the tactical map, scoreboard and map vote (for example "Villa 2.1").

## Launcher 3.0.3
- Patch notes cards now show the update number ("Update 0.39.0") instead of the same cut-off title three times.
- Changing the featured story no longer leaves the old headline showing through the new one.
