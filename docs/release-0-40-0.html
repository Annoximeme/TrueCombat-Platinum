TrueCombat: Platinum 0.40.0

Our biggest update yet. A new map, a rebuilt interface, a new generation of lighting and effects, remastered soldiers and maps, Season 1, location pings, and a long list of changes shaped by your feedback.

Interface, rebuilt

  • Every menu redesigned: main menu, Options, Deployment, server browser, loading screen and match screens.
  • New in-game HUD that defaults to 70% size and scales correctly for your resolution.
  • Death recap: see who got you, with what, and from where.
  • Post-match screen with your match summary.
  • Server browser filters, notification toasts, background blur, graphics presets, player portraits and a new cursor.
  • Map callouts now appear on screen and in pings.

Launcher 3.2

  • Optional Remastered and HD texture downloads, with per-file repair and resume.
  • Detects a graphics card change and offers to re-tune your settings.
  • Season card on the home screen.
  • New Credits and licences page.

Graphics

  • Dynamic lights: muzzle flashes and explosions light up the world around them.
  • New atmosphere and sky: sun-lit haze and a sky that matches each map's time of day (DX12).
  • Bounce light: sunlit surfaces now spill soft colour into nearby rooms and corners (DX12).
  • Lamp shadows, lit fog and pools of light under fittings.
  • Decal relief: bullet holes and scorch marks now have depth.
  • New particle system for impacts, smoke, sparks and debris.
  • Denser smoke and brass casings that stay on the floor.
  • Kill cam: depth of field and a cinematic colour grade during the replay (DX12).
  • Faster loading with a shader cache, and a smarter texture budget for lower-memory cards.
  • Every visual effect is now on by default on Ranked too: sun shafts, glare, atmosphere, bounce light, map colour mood, lit fog, and lens frost and drops. You can still turn each one off in Options. Smoke lit by muzzle flashes and full-strength weather now apply on Ranked too, the same as on Unranked.

Remastered or Classic: your choice

  • One switch in Options between the remastered look and the original TrueCombat look.
  • Remastered map materials and props, faithful to the originals.
  • Optional HD texture pack (about 1 GB), offered on Ultra in the launcher.
  • Remastered Special Forces and Terrorist soldiers with new vests and gear, rounded first-person hands and remastered items.
  • Each team now fields one soldier model, so every player reads the same in a firefight.

Season 1 †

  • Ranks from Rookie to Platinum, earned by playing.
  • Weekly contracts with clear goals.
  • Solo challenges: the Range Run and a bot challenge, each with its own leaderboard.
  • Frontline badge for everyone who plays the launch season.

New map: Lockgate

  • A drained canal lock with a pump house and a rail swing bridge, built for fair, fast rounds: mirror-matched lanes, cover and spawn times for both teams.

Location pings

  • New ping wheel: Go, Enemy, Danger, Watch, Help, Bomb, Acknowledge and Clear.
  • Pings use the map's callout names and show as dots on the minimap.
  • Bots respond to your pings.

CQB joins the core game

  • CQB weapons, optics and sounds graduate into the core game: no experimental server needed.
  • The Harbour map joins the map pool, and CQB Rules becomes a regular Unranked mode.
  • G3 semi-automatic mode, plus fixes for the M110.
  • CQB weapons enter Ranked, except the Shorty shotguns and the Akimbo M1911, which stay Unranked for now.

Combat and movement

  • Injuries now matter: a hit to the legs stops sprinting and jumping and makes you limp; a hit to the torso widens your aim and slows stamina recovery.
  • New M4-family reload animation on the remastered hands.
  • New third-person sprint pose.
  • Choose your look in Deployment: six uniforms for the special forces, five for the Terrorists, plus a cap, headscarf or balaclava for the Terrorists (Snow keeps its winter kit).
  • Red dot sight: fit an M68 to ten rifles and SMGs from the Deployment menu.

Your feedback

  • Aiming down sights: the weapon sits closer, the iron sight is larger and easier to read, and blood and hit markers no longer cover your target.
  • Recoil: less vertical climb and a faster return between targets, starting with the AK family.
  • Sniper scopes zoom in faster and zoom out much faster.
  • Fixed shots with the R93 missing while leaning left in the scope.
  • Hit confirmation: new hit sounds, with a heavy, fleshy thwack on body hits, a bone crack on headshots and a heavier kill confirm (Remastered).
  • Hit sound and hit marker stay your choice on Unranked servers and are always off on Ranked. Options shows "Hitmarkers disabled in ranked" while you play Ranked.

New players

  • Boot Camp: a ten-minute training map that teaches the basics.
  • First-launch benchmark that picks the right graphics preset for your PC.

Sound

  • Living maps: ambience beds for every map.
  • Gunfire changes with distance, from a sharp crack close by to a distant rumble.
  • More footstep variety on every surface.
  • Updated audio library for clean, stable sound at high sample rates.

Bots

  • Bots play with distinct roles and pick weapons that suit the map.
  • Bots vault and escape ledges instead of getting stuck.
  • Fixes for bots on Chlite, Villa and Shozen.
  • Bots fill empty slots and leave smoothly as players join.

Servers

  • Official servers use a 20-second map vote.

Security

  • We've tightened and expanded our security measures.

Studio

  • Sharper, high-resolution TrueCombat: Platinum logo in the launcher and menus.
  • Credits now name the ET: Legacy team and id Software, whose open-source work the Platinum engine is built on.

Fixes

  • Fixed shadows and lighting from the previous map showing on the next one after a map change, such as dark rectangles floating on walls (DX12).
  • Players can no longer be spawned in the enemy half of the map when their own spawn points are crowded.
  • First-person muzzle flashes are now clearly visible in daylight and start at the end of the barrel, including on pistols and akimbo pistols.
  • Shots that only graze a player no longer stop the bullet, show blood or count as a hit without doing damage. They now pass by.
  • Only our own official servers can show the OFFICIAL and RANKED tags in the server browser.
  • Plus many smaller fixes and polish across menus, HUD, graphics and gameplay.
