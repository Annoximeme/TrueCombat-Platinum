Free means free: how support works

TrueCombat: Platinum now has a support page and a Ko-fi. Why we added it, where the money goes, and the rules we set for ourselves so that supporting never changes how the game plays.

In this post
  1. Where the money goes
  2. The rules we set for ourselves
  3. How it works
  4. Why Ko-fi
  5. Helping without spending

Platinum is free. There is no store, no battle pass and nothing to unlock with money, and that is not going to change. But the game does not run on goodwill alone. The official servers, the master server behind the in-game browser, this website and the live leaderboards all run on rented hardware, every hour of every day.

For anyone who wants to help with those costs, there is now a way. As of today, there is a Support page and a page on Ko-fi.

Where the money goes

Running Platinum costs about €150 a month, which is also our goal on Ko-fi. That covers the official servers and the master server, the infrastructure behind the website, leaderboards and updates, the truecombat.eu domain, development time, and the tools the work needs next, such as a code-signing certificate for the installer.

The money is spent in a fixed order. Running costs come first. Then we build a reserve of three months, so the servers stay up through a quiet month. Only after that does it go to development time and tools. After every month we publish what came in, what went out and what is left in reserve. You can find the full breakdown and the monthly report under Where the money goes.

The rules we set for ourselves

Before adding a single button, we wrote down what supporting the project would never do. Those rules are on the Support page, and we hold ourselves to them:

  • No store. There is nothing to buy in the game. No skins for sale, no loot boxes, no premium currency.
  • No battle pass. Every weapon, map and mode is unlocked from the first match, for every player.
  • No advantages. A contribution never buys a better weapon, a faster rank or a place in the queue.
  • No pressure. No pop-ups, no reminders in the menus, no countdowns.

Supporting the project changes nothing about how the game plays. Someone who never contributes plays on exactly the same terms as someone who does.

How it works

You can leave a one-off contribution of any size, by card or PayPal, without creating an account. If you would rather help every month, there is a small monthly option that you can cancel whenever you like. Ko-fi handles the payment, so we never see your card details.

As a thank-you, supporters get the Supporter role on our Discord. It is a thank-you and nothing more. It has no effect in the game, on the servers or in matchmaking.

Why Ko-fi

We looked at several options. Ko-fi takes no cut of one-off contributions, works without an account for the person giving, and connects to Discord so the Supporter role arrives on its own. It also keeps things simple on our side, which matters for a small team that would rather spend its time on the game.

Helping without spending

Money is one way to help, and it is not the most important one. Playing, bringing friends along, hosting a community server and reporting bugs on Discord all help just as much. A full server on a Friday evening does more for the game than any contribution.

You will find the Support button in the top bar of every page on this site. That is where it stays: visible if you look for it, never in your way when you do not.
