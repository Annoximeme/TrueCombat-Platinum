01

Getting started

What is TrueCombat: Platinum?

A native 64-bit Windows rebuild of TrueCombat: Elite, the realistic modern-combat total conversion for Enemy Territory. It has a DirectX 12 renderer, the original maps, soldiers and HUD, and a launcher that hosts servers and installs signed updates.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no store and nothing to unlock with money, and every weapon is available from your first match. Download it and play.

Do I need TrueCombat: Elite or Enemy Territory installed?

No. The download contains everything needed to play.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a DirectX 12 graphics card and about 2.3 GB of free disk space. Older cards can use Safe start, which runs the game on OpenGL.

How do I install it?

Download the installer from the download page, choose a folder and let the launcher fetch the game. After that the launcher keeps the game and itself current.

I'm new. Where do I start?

Read the beginner's guide for the controls, game modes and a few tips that keep you alive longer. The weapons page lists every gun, and the maps page has an overhead layout of every map.

02

Playing online

Can I play online?

Yes. Two official EU servers run around the clock: Official #1 is ranked, and Official #2 is unranked with bots filling the empty slots. Both are in the launcher's Servers tab, next to every other public server.

Can I host my own server?

Yes. Press Host a server in the launcher and pick the mode, map rotation and player limit. Switch on List publicly to appear on the server list. Players outside your home network need UDP port 27971 forwarded to the hosting PC.

How do the leaderboards work?

Every completed match on a ranked server counts towards your rank and rating. You appear on the leaderboards after your first completed match, and each player gets a profile page with weapons, recent matches and rating history.

Is there a mode for clan matches?

Yes. Match mode adds ready-up, a knife round for sides, half-time and tactical timeouts that freeze the whole match. It is built into every server.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Practice offline with bots in the launcher starts a match in one click, with no connection needed.

Where do I find players?

Join the TrueCombat: Platinum Discord. That's where matches get organised, servers get shared and new versions are announced first.

03

Graphics and performance

DirectX 12 won't start. What now?

Press Safe start in the launcher. It runs the game on OpenGL in a 1280 × 720 window, and the game also falls back to OpenGL by itself if DirectX 12 fails.

Can I keep the original look?

Yes. The Low preset is the original look, with stock filtering and no added effects. High is the default, and Ultra adds modern lighting and real-time sun shadows. You can switch at any time from the launcher.

Does it support DLSS?

Yes. NVIDIA DLSS and DLAA are optional on RTX cards. Everything else runs on any DirectX 12 card.

Does it support ultrawide monitors?

Yes. The field of view widens on 21:9 and 32:9 screens instead of cropping, and the HUD stays centred.

Is there a Linux version?

The dedicated server runs natively on Linux, and both official servers use it. The game itself is for Windows.

04

The project

Is this complete TC:E?

Very nearly. Every weapon, all seven game modes, the original movement speed, leaning, ladders and swimming are in. The roadmap shows what is playable now and what follows.

Is it an official TrueCombat release?

No. TrueCombat: Elite was created by Team Terminator. TrueCombat: Platinum is a fan project by Team Terminus and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original team.

Who makes it?

Team Terminus: two TrueCombat: Elite veterans. RaziC* leads development and RavoC leads the community.

How do I follow development?

The devlog explains what is being built and why, the patch notes list every change, and new versions are announced on Discord first.

How can I support the project?

Playing and bringing a friend helps most. If you want to chip in towards the servers, the support page shows where every euro goes. Nothing in the game is sold.

I want to write about it

Grab the press kit with logos, unedited screenshots and a fact sheet.

Still stuck? Ask in the TrueCombat: Platinum Discord. The team reads every question.
