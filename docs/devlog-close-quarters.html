Close quarters, together

CQB took TC:E into close quarters with new weapons, detailed models and fresh ideas. We are bringing all of it into Platinum, starting as an experimental playlist you can try long before anything touches Ranked.

In this post
  1. What CQB brings
  2. New sidearms
  3. The new weapons in game
  4. Harbour
  5. CQB Rules
  6. Feel and detail
  7. Why Experimental
  8. How we are testing it

In December 2010, GrooveSix Studios and Team Terminator released TrueCombat: CQB, a close-quarters take on TC:E with new weapons, far more detailed models and a set of ideas about how a modern tactical shooter should feel. Its last release was alpha 0.223, in April 2011.

Platinum is built on TC:E. CQB grew from the same roots. Bringing the two together is the natural next step, and 0.38.0 is where it starts.

Everything from CQB arrives as Experimental. Ranked plays exactly as it does today until each piece has earned its place.

What CQB brings

CQB is smaller than its reputation. It is an alpha with one map and one mode, but nearly every model in it was rebuilt from scratch. A first-person AK47 in TC:E has 777 triangles. CQB's has 6,698. The MP5 goes from 820 to 8,705.

Here is everything we are bringing over:

  • 14 new weapons, among them the SCAR, G3, M110, MP9, Mini Uzi, USP, Wilson Combat 1911, SG 552 and a sawn-off double-barrel shotgun, plus akimbo pistols.
  • Upgraded models for the weapons Platinum already has, and new gear for the player models: helmets with goggles and masks, pouches and radios.
  • Harbour, CQB's only map, rebuilt for Platinum.
  • CQB Rules, a fast team mode with perks and health regeneration.
  • Movement and feel: ledge grabbing, smarter spawns and smoother movement around corners.
  • Optics, sound and impacts: new scope reticles, a red dot, radio callouts, indoor and outdoor gunfire reverb, and bullet holes for plastic.

New sidearms

CQB replaced several of TC:E's pistols with new, far more detailed ones, and added the Shorty. These are studio renders of the first-person models, lit in a neutral scene so you can see the detail.

The new weapons in game

Here is every new CQB weapon as it looks in Platinum today, in first person and on the player models. All of them are part of the experimental set, with stats based on TC:E weapons of the same calibre. One thing is not right yet: with some of these weapons the support hand hovers below the barrel instead of gripping it, in first person and on the player models. It is a known issue and we are fixing it.

Harbour

Harbour is a dockside district of warehouses, container yards, alleys and basements, with rain and city ambience. It suits the way CQB was meant to be played: short sightlines, lots of doors and constant decisions about which corner to clear first.

To bring it into Platinum we gave it a tactical map and minimap, loading screen artwork and modern materials for 61 of its surfaces, so it holds up next to our other maps in DirectX 12.

CQB Rules

CQB Rules is a fast team mode built around CQB's ideas. You respawn after one second, and a round ends at 20 kills or 20 minutes. The first kill of the match is called out as First Blood.

Before you deploy, you pick one perk from each of three groups:

  • Body: Lightweight (faster running and sprinting), Protected (an improved vest) or Tank (a heavy vest that costs you speed).
  • Skill: Steady Aim (less spread and sway), Commando (more knife damage, and you can grab ledges) or Quick Reload.
  • Gear: Extra Ammo, a Muzzle Attachment (suppressor, or a wider spread for shotguns) or High-grade Ammo (better penetration, and slugs for shotguns).

Health comes back on its own if you stay out of fire for three seconds, but only partly. Take a heavy hit and you will recover to 80, 64 or 50 percent, never back to full. It rewards breaking contact without making you invulnerable.

Movement is tuned the way CQB had it: a little faster on foot, shorter jumps, and heavier weapons slow you down more.

Feel and detail

Some of CQB's smaller ideas apply everywhere the experimental switch is on:

  • Smarter spawns. You respawn at the point farthest from living enemies, so you are not dropped into a fight you cannot win.
  • Ledge grabbing with the Commando perk. Harbour has its own list of places you cannot climb, so nobody ends up on a roof or outside the map.
  • Smoother corners. CQB's movement change that stops you snagging on door frames and pillar edges.
  • New optics. CQB's scope reticles and the M68 red dot.
  • Sound. Radio callouts for contact, enemy down and man down, and gunfire that echoes differently indoors and outdoors.
  • Impacts. Bullets now leave proper holes in plastic.

Why Experimental

CQB was an alpha, and parts of it were never finished. Some of its numbers had to be our best first guess, and a perk that feels fine in one round can break a match over twenty. So everything from CQB sits behind one switch that server owners can turn on. Ranked servers always keep it off.

When the switch is on, you will see an EXPERIMENTAL tag on the HUD, on the loading screen, on the mode name and on every item that comes from CQB. Stats from those matches are marked as experimental, so they never mix with Ranked.

Each piece then earns its way out on its own. When a weapon, the map or a perk is up to the standard of the rest of Platinum, it joins the normal game. The rest stays experimental until it is ready.

How we are testing it

Every phase of the merge builds cleanly for Windows and for the Linux server, and the rules behind regeneration, perks and spawns have their own automated checks. CQB went through the same combined test pass as the rest of 0.38.0: every map, both renderers, both HUD styles, with the switch off and on.

Credit where it is due

TrueCombat: CQB was made by GrooveSix Studios and Team Terminator. The weapons, gear, map and many of the ideas in this post are theirs, and they are credited in the game's Credits screen. We are grateful for the work they put into keeping TrueCombat moving forward.

If you play CQB Rules or Harbour on a server that runs them and something feels off, tell us on Discord or press F12 in game. Experimental only works if you tell us what you think.
