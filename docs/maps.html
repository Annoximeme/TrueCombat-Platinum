Game modes

How the maps are played

Objective and team modes

  • Flag capture. Steal the enemy backpack and carry it home. First team to 3 wins.
  • Bomb. Terrorists plant at site A or B, SpecOps defend or defuse. 5 minute rounds.
  • Team deathmatch. First team to 20 frags.

Core modes

  • Practice with knock-down targets.
  • Elimination and Team elimination.
  • Point capture.

All seven modes are playable today. Pick one under Hosting in the launcher.
