v0.39.7NieuwsteA focused update: a proper bomb plant, a second chance at your loadout, and the first round of fixes after 0.39.6.#74 · 0.39.7-sandbox44 · 20 nov 2026

Bomb planting

  • Planting is now a deliberate action: hold Use, or hold fire with the C4, inside a bomb site for three seconds.
  • Your soldier kneels and arms the charge, and every player nearby sees and hears it happen.
  • The charge is set down at your feet instead of being thrown. Let go early and the plant is cancelled.

Deployment

  • Changed your mind after spawning? Pick new weapons in Deployment and they are issued on the spot, as long as you have not moved, fired or taken damage.
  • Fixed the Deployment menu opening on the Terrorists after joining or a map change, which could move Spec Ops players to the other team.

Sound

Security

  • We've tightened and expanded our security measures.
  • TrueCombat: Platinum now needs an internet connection to start.

Fixes

  • Wet ground now follows the real roof cover: streets no longer dry out and soak again as you walk through a doorway, and puddles follow the shape of the ground (DX12).
  • Other players stay on their sights in third person while firing aimed, instead of dropping to the hip-fire pose.
  • Night maps: player and object shadows now follow the moon's real brightness, so shadows no longer float on moonlit streets (DX12).
  • Players who respawn mid-fall no longer leave a body frozen half-standing; the body finishes its fall and lies down.
  • The AK-74M Spetsnaz red dot sight shows its dot again.
  • Fixed the arm holding the C4 drawing in flat orange on OpenGL while planting.

v0.39.6A full weapon balance pass, a cinematic kill cam, sharper aiming, real rain, and a long list of fixes you asked for.#73 · 0.39.6-sandbox43 · 20 nov 2026

Weapon balance

  • Rifles are deadlier up close: two body shots, and one head shot at normal fighting distances.
  • Pistols are sidearms again: three body shots up close, four at range. They are the fastest guns to raise and move with.
  • Damage now falls off with distance. SMGs and machine pistols rule doorways, shotguns rule rooms, rifles hold the street, and snipers barely lose a thing.
  • Each rifle kicks in its own way, from the light M4 to the heavy, climbing AK-47.
  • Aiming takes longer with heavier guns: 150 ms for pistols, around 250 ms for rifles, up to 420 ms for bolt-action snipers.
  • Taps and short bursts stay pinpoint. Hold the trigger past three rounds and your aimed spread opens up.
  • Walking while aiming widens your spread. Stand or crouch to win the long fight.
  • R93 and SR-8 still kill with one aimed hit, but a magazine reload now takes 3 seconds, so a miss costs more.
  • Matching Spec Ops and Terrorist weapons are within 8 percent of each other in time to kill at close range.
  • Server owners can return to the classic numbers with g_weaponBalance 0.

Kill cam

  • The round-ending replay is now a short, cut edit: a wide shot when both players are in view, over the shoulder, then the victim.
  • The killing shot slows to a crawl, with its tracers, muzzle flashes, impacts and gunfire replayed in time.
  • Sound muffles in and opens up on the shot, framed by letterbox bars, the round result and a lower third naming the killer, weapon and distance.

Aiming

  • Focus now zooms in further. Scopes are unchanged.
  • Aiming down sights and focus darken the edges of the screen and soften the periphery, on DX12 and OpenGL, the same for every player.

Sound

  • Real rain: a full stereo rain bed outdoors, and rain drumming on roofs and windows when you're inside, rising and falling with the storm.
  • Dripping water only plays once the rain has stopped.
  • Running out of breath now ends in an audible gasp, and breathing is louder.

Gameplay

  • Sprinting tires you faster, and holding focus now drains stamina and lets go when you're out of breath.
  • Reloading now ends aiming on every weapon. Scoped rifles drop the scope, iron sights return to the hip.
  • Bolt-action rifles (R93, SR-8) now play the full bolt cycle after each shot and after a reload.

Fixes

  • Bodies now stay for 30 seconds in every mode before sinking away, instead of vanishing on respawn.
  • Pressing Use at a door opens the door, rather than picking up a weapon lying nearby.
  • The dropped magazine no longer pops up mid-screen during a reload, most noticeable on ultrawide displays.
  • Bullet holes and other decals no longer flicker.
  • Player and prop shadows no longer appear on indoor walls (DX12).

v0.39.5The weather, tracer and sound update: dry interiors, brighter rounds, and a sound engine you can actually hear.#72 · 0.39.5-sandbox42 · 19 nov 2026

Weather

  • Rain and snow now stay outdoors. Every drop checks for open sky above it, so nothing falls under eaves, canopies, shed roofs or moving roof sections.
  • Interiors stay dry, with only the odd puddle. Step back outside and the ground is wet again. There is also a little less standing water outdoors.
  • Cleaner wet-ground reflections on DX12: no more smeared copies of players or poles, and your own weapon is never reflected.
  • New server setting g_weatherOff: server owners can leave out weather types (for example storm or snow) or switch off individual effects such as lightning, the screen flash, thunder, splashes, wet ground, sky, colour grading, weather sounds, gusts or precipitation.

Sound

  • The new sound layers now play with the Original weapon sounds as well: rounds cracking past, casings and magazines that sound different on every surface, room tails and impact variations follow the Remastered setting instead of the weapon sound choice.
  • Rooms ring the way they are built. Room size and wall materials shape the echo: a concrete room rings long, a wooden house sounds warmer and carpeted or snowy spaces stay dull. Reverb is stronger in Remastered.
  • Gunfire carries through doorways and around corners, including a door, along a hallway and through the next door. Walls and distance dampen sound more than before. This works the same for every player.
  • Doppler: rounds, running players and thrown grenades shift in pitch as they pass.
  • 3D headphone audio is now on by default for stereo devices when Sound output is set to Automatic. Choose Stereo speakers in Options > Audio to turn it off.

Visuals

  • Tracers burn brighter and wider, and light up the walls and floors around them more strongly.

Fixes

  • Weapons now lower only when the muzzle is actually against a wall. Walking along a wall or leaning past a door frame keeps your weapon up, and a lowered weapon can no longer fire.

v0.39.4The weather update: storms, rain and snow roll in, tracers burn brighter, and the maps get real shadow.#71 · 0.39.4-sandbox41 · 19 nov 2026

Weather

  • Every map now rolls its own weather when it loads: clear skies, overcast, rain, thunderstorms, the soaked calm after the rain, snow or high wind. Everyone on the server sees the same sky.
  • Thunderstorms bring heavy rain, lightning over the rooftops and thunder that arrives after the flash.
  • Rain falls in fine streaks and stays outside: step under a roof and you hear it drumming overhead instead. Wet ground shines on DX12.
  • Northport is the stormiest map, the snow maps get snowfall, and the loading screen tells you what's coming.
  • Ranked stays fair: rain and snow are capped so nothing hides a player, and the lightning flash is toned down.

Weapon sounds

  • Original weapon sounds are the default again. The remastered set is now called Experimental (Options > Audio > Weapon sounds) and is now louder and harder-hitting, without the low kick. The odd ricochet whines are gone.
  • With Experimental sounds, passing rounds crack and whizz past your ear, and casings and dropped magazines sound different on concrete, wood, dirt and snow.

Sound

  • Walls now muffle sound by what they're made of: you hear more through a wooden door than through stone.
  • New Sound output setting (Options > Audio): Automatic, Headphones (3D), Stereo speakers, 5.1 or 7.1 surround.
  • Grenades and charges hit harder: a deep low end, falling debris, a far-off rumble, and a pressure thump when they go off indoors.
  • Wind gusts outdoors, draughts and creaking indoors on the snow maps.
  • The sound engine uses less CPU in busy fights.

Visuals

  • Tracers: now every third round, longer, wider and brighter, lighting up walls and floors as they pass and where they hit.
  • Darker, moodier lighting: shadowed corners are darker and lit areas stand out.

Fixes

  • Bots no longer let go of ladders halfway up and fall back down.

v0.39.3The sound update: remastered gunfire, a smarter sound engine, free aim and two-handed AKs again.#70 · 0.39.3-sandbox40 · 19 nov 2026

Weapon sounds

  • Remastered weapon sounds: every gun gets its own louder, punchier shot with a real kick, and its echo now matches where it's fired (room, hall, street or open snow). Prefer the classic sound? Options > Audio > Weapon sounds > Original.
  • Bullet impacts get up to 12 variations per surface, and they never play the same sound twice in a row. Dirt, sand, snow and grass hits are now audible, ricochets whine off hard surfaces, and near-miss cracks have more variety.

Sound engine

  • A gun fired inside a building now echoes in that building, even when you're outside.
  • Sound finds its way through doors and windows, so you hear a fight from the doorway it's coming from.
  • In heavy firefights the loudest shots stay clear and quieter sounds step back a little.
  • Distant shots arrive a moment later, like real sound, the same for every player. Shots near walls slap back off them.
  • More sounds can play at once, at 48 kHz.
  • A gunshot right next to you briefly dulls your hearing.
  • Footsteps far away echo in the room they're in.

Aim and animation

  • New free aim option (Options > Mouse & View, off by default): at the hip your gun moves inside a small deadzone before the view turns, and aiming down the sights brings the sights to it. The TC:E classic mode is still there.
  • Fixed the AK-47 and G3 rifles being held with one hand in third person.

v0.39.0Smarter opponents, four classic community maps, a rebuilt launcher and a deeper layer of atmosphere on every map.#67 · 0.39.0-sandbox37 · 18 nov 2026

Smarter bots

  • Bots now listen. Footsteps and gunfire give away your position, and they remember where they last saw you.
  • Bomb mode plays like a real match: attackers agree on a plan and can cover their entry with smoke, defenders hold sites and regroup to retake together.
  • Bots choose when to hold, push or fall back, and vary their peeks instead of rushing in one line.
  • Teams learn which round plans work on each map and adapt over a session.
  • The end of a round no longer causes a brief hitch on servers with bots.
  • Bots with sniper rifles now use the scope on every difficulty.
  • Bots no longer freeze in front of you while you aim down a scope. If they can't get out of your line of fire, they crouch and keep moving.
  • Bots no longer climb the same ledge over and over, and the bomb carrier no longer keeps switching between sites.
  • Bots that got stuck at the start of their route, or somewhere with no route (a roof, a pit by a ladder), now find their way out.
  • One bot keeps the defuse or bomb pickup job, and bots no longer stand inside you when you stop.
  • Bots walk quietly when they know an enemy is close, and round starts with many bots are smoother.

Community maps

  • Four of the most played TrueCombat:Elite community maps return: Villa 2.1, Shozen, Snowtown and Chlite, playable on Unranked servers.
  • Several of their original sounds have been cleaned up and levelled to sit alongside our own ambience.
  • Stadtrand gets its second bomb site back: site A now works on every server, Ranked included.
  • Community maps have minimaps, tactical maps and loading screens with a map plan, picture and description.
  • Bots pick up the flag on Villa 2.1 in Flag Capture.
  • The site A crate on Villa 2.1 breaks when the bomb goes off.

Interface and match screens

  • Clearer match screens: map names read properly on the scoreboard and tactical map, round results name the teams ("Terrorists win the round"), and Bomb, Flag and Capture Point objective cards say what to do next.
  • Capture Point: the point is marked on the tactical map and the minimap.
  • Team Deathmatch and Flag: waiting players see "Deploying in N" instead of "No more deployments this round".
  • Spectators of team matches get the team scoreboard and the right status texts.
  • The firing range panel no longer overlaps the minimap and says how many targets are up. The FPS counter no longer sits on the minimap.
  • Small in-game UI sizes keep all text readable.
  • Match end: the podium is lit the same on every map, and the game view dims behind the map vote.
  • Kill feed, chat box, vote menu, weapon pickup prompt and first-launch tips no longer overlap or cut off text.
  • Flash grenades also blind the minimap, compass and objective card.
  • The final-kill replay camera no longer ends up inside the shooter or behind a fence.

Graphics

  • Smoother baked lighting, with soft shadow edges on walls and floors.
  • Trees, bushes, grass and canvas sway gently in the wind.
  • Sun shafts and soft glare when looking toward the sun (DX12).
  • Wet patches on the ground that reflect the world around them (DX12, Ultra).
  • Water ripples from players and gunfire.
  • Higher-detail fire and explosions, and smoke lit briefly by blasts and muzzle flashes.
  • Brick, cobblestone, gravel and rock gain real depth up close, from height maps that match their lighting (DX12, Ultra).
  • Aiming down sights softens the rear of your own weapon while the sights stay sharp (DX12).
  • Frost forms at the edges of your view in the cold on Snow and fades indoors.
  • Each map gets its own subtle colour mood.
  • Ranked servers and clan matches keep sun shafts, glare, colour mood, lens frost and lit smoke off, so every player sees the same picture.

Combat feel and immersion

  • Focus zoom. While aiming, hold the right mouse button to steady your view with a slight extra zoom. Release to return to your normal sight picture. With hold-to-aim, the sprint key focuses instead.
  • Gear rustles and magazines rattle as you move, and the last round in the magazine ends with a dry click.
  • At low health you hear a slow heartbeat that quickens as you near death, sound dulls and colour drains from the world.
  • After a long sprint you catch your breath.
  • Fallen soldiers settle naturally: bodies fall away from walls, keep a little of their momentum and come to rest along slopes and stairs.

Player models

  • Your sidearm now rides on your right thigh, your knife on your left hip and your grenades on your belt, so everyone can see what you carry.
  • The support hand now sits on the handguard on every two-handed weapon in third person.
  • When spectating another player, their weapon now moves with them: sprint, aim, sway and bob are all visible.

Launcher 3.0.2

  • A completely rebuilt launcher: live server cards on the Play page, a new server browser with favourites, recent servers and search, and news with full articles.
  • Friends. Add friends by code, see who is playing, and join or invite in one click. Optional and off until you turn it on.
  • Downloads panel with pause and resume, a dark tray menu with quick play, and update notifications.
  • New Accessibility settings: high contrast, larger text, colour-blind friendly markers and reduced motion.
  • The launcher is now also available in Dutch.
  • Includes the repair improvements from launcher 2.6.2: missing or damaged files are downloaded again automatically, and errors now say what went wrong.

CQB (experimental)

  • CQB spawn rules now apply only in the CQB Rules playlist. Every other mode keeps standard spawns.
  • Official #2 (Unranked) now runs with experimental content on: the new CQB weapons are in every match, and Harbour joins the rotation as Team Deathmatch.
  • CQB Rules: health regeneration and the Protected and Tank perks are no longer undone by the next hit.

Fixes

  • Fixed game sound fading away or turning into thumps a few seconds into a match on audio devices set to high sample rates (96 kHz and above).
  • Bomb rounds are no longer scored twice when the explosion takes someone with it.
  • The bomb's explosion now hurts everyone nearby and counts for the player who planted it.
  • SpecOps can no longer plant a bomb they picked up.
  • Blocked rotating doors no longer swing backwards on servers.
  • Grenades and smoke from the previous round are cleared when a new round starts.
  • Players no longer slide down slopes while standing still, and stay planted when running downhill.
  • Pulling the pin on a grenade is now audible to you and nearby players.
  • Northport's harbour ambience plays again. It had been silent since the original release.
  • Smoother movement along the glass fronts on Railhouse, and fewer small freezes the first time a sound or effect is used.
  • A "Connection interrupted" notice appears when the server stops answering.
  • Recorded demos play back without menus opening over them.
  • The M4 SOPMOD sight shows a single red dot at 1440p and 4K.
  • Bug reports (F12) at high resolutions no longer say the screenshot is too large to upload.
  • The Deployment menu closes when the match ends.
  • Replaced an unused system sound that shipped with the original game files.
  • We've tightened and expanded our security measures.

v0.38.0Our biggest update yet. Content from TrueCombat: CQB arrives on an experimental front, you can now take weapons off the battlefield, there's a firing range, and a long list of changes makes every firefight hit harder.#64 · 0.38.0-sandbox34 · 17 nov 2026

Experimental: truecombat: cqb comes to platinum

  • Harbour. A new close-quarters map, fully rebuilt for DX12 with its own minimap and tactical map.
  • Experimental Arsenal. Thirteen weapons enter testing, including the M1911, USP .40, Shorty, MP9, Mini Uzi, SCAR, SG 552, REC7, G3 and the M110 marksman rifle, with dual M1911s and dual Shorties. Find them under the new Experimental tab in the Deployment Menu.
  • CQB Rules. A fast-paced playlist with Bodycount (first team to 20 kills, quick respawns), perks such as Lightweight, Protected, Tank and Commando, and health that recovers after a few seconds out of the fight.
  • New movement. Commando can grab ledges and climb, corner sliding keeps you moving along walls, and spawns place you away from the enemy.
  • New optics and sound. ACOG, EOTech, Leupold and M68 sights, a new radio voice and indoor reverb.

New features

  • Battlefield pickups. Weapons now drop when a player falls. Walk up to an enemy's weapon and press Use to swap it for your own, with whatever ammunition its owner left behind. Enabled on official Ranked and Unranked servers.
  • Firing range. Choose Firing range from the Play menu to sight in every weapon on targets from 10 to 100 metres. Each hit shows where it landed, the damage and the distance, with your accuracy for the session.
  • Vaulting. Press jump at a low wall, crate or window to vault over it or climb on top. The classic mantle stays as it was.
  • Know when to jump in. With the launcher in the background, Windows lets you know when people are playing on a public server. One click and you're in. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Female soldier option. Choose Male or Female under Options > Customise Game > Player. Both use the same hitboxes, so the choice is purely visual.

Aim and performance

  • Aiming down sights now zooms and changes sensitivity the instant you press the key, with no round-trip delay.
  • New Aim speed option: keep the Classic feel, or match your aim sensitivity to your hip-fire feel.
  • New Frame Rate Limit option in Options > System, from 60 to 500 FPS. The launcher no longer forces 125 FPS.
  • Movement, stamina, recoil recovery and jumping now behave the same at every frame rate.
  • Lower input latency on DX12, with tighter frame pacing.

Firefights

  • Explosions now shake the camera by distance, throw dust from the ceiling, scorch the ground and leave dirt on your screen.
  • Indoor haze, drifting dust, falling leaves and birds that scatter at the first shot bring every map to life.
  • Impacts react to what they hit: sparks on metal, splinters on wood, glass that breaks properly, and water splashes.
  • Blood spreads into pools, uniforms pick up blood and dirt, and players flinch when hit and fall according to where they were hit.
  • The death camera now drops and rolls with the body before fading out.
  • A replay of the final kill closes out each round.
  • New first-person touches: a dip when you land, the gun pulls back at walls, and spent magazines drop on reload.
  • Footprints in the snow, muzzle flashes that cast shadows, heat shimmer from hot barrels and gunfire that sounds different indoors and at range.
  • Combat voice lines: soldiers call out contacts, kills and casualties.
  • An optional Auto-hide HUD fades the HUD out when you're not in the fight. It is off by default.

HUD

  • Minimap. Choose a round (new default) or square minimap in Options.
  • Bomb mode. Everyone sees "Bomb planted at &lt;location&gt;" when the bomb goes down. A new bomb card shows the site, a countdown and defuse progress, and the carrier gets clear hints, including a warning when they try to plant outside a site.
  • Hit and suppression effects now fade smoothly at the screen edges instead of showing dark bands.
  • Kill messages show long weapon names in full, for example the AK-74M Spetsnaz.
  • Grenades now appear by their proper names: M67, M84 and M83.
  • The game window is now titled TrueCombat: Platinum.

Weapons and visuals

  • Upgraded first-person weapon models and materials, with sharper metal, wear and texture detail on DX12.
  • New player gear for both teams.

Bots

  • Bots vary their routes in Bomb and Elimination, so rounds no longer play out the same way twice.
  • When a player drops out mid-match, a bot holds their place until they return.

Launcher and installer

  • New Play page artwork captured in-game.
