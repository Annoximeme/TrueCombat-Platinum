v0.32.0NieuwsteA major update. Movement now runs at the original TC:E pace, and leaning, ladders, swimming and spectating are back. This release also brings a competitive match mode, smarter bots on every objective, a new-player welcome, a one-click installer, and a fix for the most common crash to desktop.#53 · 0.32.0-sandbox23 · 7 nov 2026

Movement and gunplay

  • Movement speed matches the original TC:E. Running, sprinting, crouching and going prone all move at the original speeds.
  • Walk and run animations now switch at the original speed, so other players move the way you remember.
  • Stamina recovers when you walk, stand still, crouch or go prone, like the original.
  • Third-person animations now follow the original TC:E set: weapon-specific stances for rifles, pistols, knife and grenades, a raise animation on weapon switch, prone pistol poses, and death animations that react to how and where a player is hit.
  • Bullet tracer lines are gone. Muzzle flashes now show correctly on every player and light up their surroundings on DirectX 12 and OpenGL.

New original content

  • Lean around corners with Q and E.
  • Climb ladders. Bots use them too.
  • Swim, dive and drown. Northport's water is fully swimmable.
  • Glass, wood and metal now shatter into debris.
  • Defusing a bomb puts the pliers in your hands.
  • When you die, the camera follows a living teammate and shows their health and ammo.
  • Suppressors: the MAC-10, MP5, M4 and AKS-74U now have a Suppressor toggle in the Deployment Menu, using the original suppressed variants and their trade-offs.

Competitive match mode

  • Organised matches with ready-up, an optional knife round, side pick, halftime side swap, tactical timeouts and a final score screen.
  • Server admins run it with the match rcon commands. Players use ready, notready, timeout, stay and swap.

Bots

  • Bots now play the objective. They plant and defuse the bomb, carry the flag home, and take and hold the capture point, with team callouts in chat (bot_chat).
  • Point Capture is fairer. The point is now placed at an equal walking distance from both spawns, and it takes 30 seconds to capture (g_captureHoldMs).
  • Fixed: a single bomb detonation could award the round to the attackers more than once.

New players

  • First launch now opens a three-step welcome: the basics, your controls, and how to start playing.
  • New in the launcher: Practice offline with bots starts a Railhouse Team Deathmatch against bots in one click.
  • New players see nine short tips during their first matches. Turn them off with cg_tips 0, or bring them back with tips_reset.

Install and updates

  • The download is now a small installer: TrueCombat-Platinum-Setup.exe. It installs the launcher, and the launcher downloads the game.
  • Choose your install folder, with desktop and Start menu shortcuts and a proper uninstaller.
  • New Versions button in the launcher: stay on an older release, or switch back to the latest at any time.

Online

  • The in-game server browser now lists official Platinum servers.
  • Ranked hosting: server owners can enter a ranked key in the launcher's Hosting tab, so their matches count towards the leaderboards.
  • Team Terminus staff now carry a verified emblem and an orange name on the scoreboard.

Interface

  • The HUD, scoreboard, Deployment Menu, map vote and main menu now fit every screen shape, from 4:3 to 32:9 super-ultrawide. Nothing gets cut off at the edges any more.

Performance

  • Removed the long freezes that happened the first time a weapon or explosion appeared in a match. Weapon and effect media now load with the map.

Fixes

  • Fixed a crash to desktop when a player was kicked or banned, or the server restarted or shut down.
  • Your team, class and loadout are now kept after a map restart or map change.
  • The cheat detection no longer flags the game's own HD and interface files.
  • The speed check no longer misfires after a freeze or alt-tab.
  • Elimination rounds now last 5 minutes, like the original (server cvar tcp_roundtime).
  • Restored missing crosshairs and server-browser icons.

Known issues

  • Easy Anti-Cheat is prepared but not switched on yet.
  • Still to come: the first-person muzzle flash, a sniper scope overlay, ledge climbing, and the modern Ultra visuals.
  • In DirectX 12, water seen from above in third person can render black.

v0.31.0The biggest update yet. The full TC:E arsenal, real recoil and penetration, three new game modes, bots, 3D sound, the Deployment Menu, end-of-match map votes, lag compensation, player ranks and server admin tools.#52 · 0.31.0-sandbox22 · 7 nov 2026

Weapons

  • The full TC:E weapon roster is in, with each team's own weapons, pistols and grenades, and semi, burst and full-auto fire modes where the original had them.
  • Original recoil and spread. Each weapon kicks, sways and settles like TC:E, aiming (zoom key) tightens your shots, and moving, jumping and landing throw your aim off.
  • Bullets go through walls, doors and crates the way they did in TC:E, losing damage with every surface. Thin cover no longer saves you.
  • Original damage, reload times and weapon-switch times for every weapon, including shell-by-shell shotgun reloads and the nine-pellet shotgun spread.
  • Knife, frag, stun and smoke grenades work again, with stun and blast effects.
  • Weapons now show on other players, with their own original firing and reload sounds.

Game modes

  • New: Team Deathmatch, Flag Capture and Bomb, alongside Practice, Elimination, Team Elimination and Point Capture. Pick them in Hosting.
  • Map objectives, timers, rotating doors, breakable glass and wood, and all map props and ambient sounds are back.
  • End-of-match map vote: when a match ends, everyone votes for the next map with the number keys. Most votes wins.

Deployment menu

  • The original Deployment Menu is back. Choose your team, class and loadout before you spawn, or reopen it any time with the limbo menu key.
  • Team balance is enforced: you cannot join the bigger team.

Bots

  • Practice and fill games with bots. Set the number of bots and their skill (1-5) in Hosting. Bots navigate every stock map, take cover, aim with human-like reaction time and fill empty slots on your server.

Sound

  • Full 3D sound: gunfire, footsteps and ambience come from where they happen and follow your view.
  • Walls muffle sound, rooms have their own echo, and distant gunfire carries across the map.
  • Original surface footsteps for stone, metal, wood, grass, snow, water and more. Walking is silent, sprinting is loud.
  • Optional headphone 3D sound (HRTF): set s_alHRTF 1.

Online play

  • Lag compensation: the server checks your shot against where players were on your screen, so hits you see are hits that count.
  • Recoil, spread and your own shots are now predicted on your PC, so firing feels instant even with high ping.
  • Fewer lost inputs on unstable connections (cl_packetdup now defaults to 2).

Ranks and stats

  • Your kills, deaths, accuracy, headshots and wins now count towards an XP rank and a skill rating, shown on the Leaderboards page of the website. No account needed.

Server hosts

  • New admin commands over rcon: kick, ban, unban, ban list, and an admin log.
  • Edit the map rotation live with maprotation add, remove and move. It is saved and survives a restart. Set rounds per map with g_roundlimit.
  • Built-in cheat detection: the server flags speed hacks, timing tricks, aim snaps and altered game files.

Performance

  • Smoother DirectX 12: render states are prepared while the map loads instead of in the middle of a fight, removing most first-time stutters.

Launcher

  • The public server list is live: hosted games that choose List publicly now show up for everyone in the Servers tab.
  • Hosting now offers Team Deathmatch, Flag Capture and Bomb, plus bot count and skill.
  • If the game crashes, the launcher offers to save a crash report you can send us, and Report a problem includes it.

Also new since 0.29.0

  • HD textures for the world, models, HUD and menus. Remove the zzz_hd_*.pk3 files from tcetest to get the original look back.
  • Low / High / Ultra graphics presets. High is the default, and Ultra adds stronger effects and NVIDIA DLAA where available.
  • The original TC:E chat box, kill feed, death notices and Tab scoreboard.
  • The M83 smoke can be thrown underhand: press the zoom key with the M83 in hand.
  • Updates now download only the files that changed.
  • The Servers tab shows the public server list. Hosts can choose List publicly in Hosting & favorites.

Known issues

  • Easy Anti-Cheat is prepared but not switched on yet.
  • Leaning, ladders, swimming and the spectator mode are still to come.
  • Your weapon choice resets after a map restart. Pick it again in the Deployment Menu.
  • Tested on one PC over local connections. Separate PCs and the internet are not verified yet.
  • Use the separate RC8 download for complete TC:E matches.

v0.29.0The launcher becomes a full hub with a living map header, a server list and in-launcher news, and movement looks smoother online.#50 · 0.29.0-sandbox20 · 5 nov 2026

Launcher

  • The header now moves slowly through real in-game shots of all six maps and cross-fades between them.
  • New Play, Servers and News tabs.
  • Servers shows your favorite and recent servers and your own server as cards with a map picture, player count and ping. Click a card to pick it, double-click or press JOIN to play, and use the star to save a favorite.
  • News shows the latest patch notes inside the launcher.
  • Hosting & favorites (formerly Servers & hosting) now lets you pick the starting map from picture cards.
  • Smoother hover effects, rounded window corners on Windows 11, a larger window on high-resolution screens and dark drop-down lists.

Updates

  • Updates are now signed by Team Terminus. The launcher only installs updates that carry a valid signature.
  • While an update downloads you can see its speed and time left, cancel it, or open What's new.

Multiplayer

  • Fewer camera jolts from small movement corrections when packets arrive late or go missing.
  • Respawns and practice resets stay instant, without a camera slide.
  • Respawning players no longer slide between their old and new positions.
  • Movement-correction smoothing can be adjusted or turned off through the console.

Known issues

  • Still unfinished: recoil, spread, penetration, leaning, ladders, swimming, aiming down sights and the original round rules.
  • There is no public server list yet. The Servers tab shows servers you have saved or joined before.
  • Tested on one PC over local connections. Separate PCs and the internet are not verified yet.
  • Use the separate RC8 download for complete TC:E matches.

v0.28.0It brings the restyled launcher with a built-in update bar, optional DLSS/DLAA, steadier startup and a new connection version.#49 · 0.28.0-sandbox19 · 5 nov 2026

Launcher

  • New title bar that blends into the artwork, with the game emblem, your version and window buttons that match the rest of the launcher.
  • New update bar above the PLAY button. It shows your version, checks for new versions, downloads and verifies them, and installs them on restart. Your settings, profile and server data are kept. It checks the official TrueCombat: Platinum releases on GitHub each time the launcher opens.
  • Servers & hosting, Advanced graphics and the graphics report now have proper headers and grouped panels.
  • Smoother corners, soft shadows, icons on the section headings and clearer drop-down lists.
  • Text stays sharp when the launcher window is resized.
  • The Advanced graphics window no longer cuts off its notes and buttons.

Graphics

  • Added optional NVIDIA DLSS and DLAA for RTX graphics cards. It is off by default. Enter r_dx12Dlss 1 in the console for DLAA, or 2 to 5 for the DLSS quality levels, then vid_restart.
  • The game now recovers more gracefully when a graphics renderer fails to start.

Multiplayer

  • New connection version. Version 0.28.0 cannot join 0.27.0 or older servers, and older versions cannot join 0.28.0 servers.
  • A clear message now explains when your game and the server do not match.

Known issues

  • Still unfinished: recoil, spread, penetration, leaning, ladders, swimming, aiming down sights and the original round rules.
  • Tested on one PC over local connections. Separate PCs and the internet are not verified yet.
  • Use the separate RC8 download for complete TC:E matches.

v0.27.0The new launcher, sprinting, crouching, going prone, stamina and head/body/leg hits.#48 · 0.27.0-sandbox18

New launcher

  • PLAY, Safe start and Open game menu.
  • Saved player name, favorite and recent servers, and a check whether a server answers.
  • Host Practice, Elimination, Team Elimination or Point Capture.

Movement

  • Sprint with Shift, crouch with C, go prone with X.
  • Running, sprinting, crouching and crawling speeds follow the original values.

Combat

  • Shots hit the head, body or legs separately, each with its own damage.
  • Crouched and prone players are smaller targets.

HUD

  • The stamina bar drains and refills as you move.
  • The stance icon shows standing, crouched or prone.

DirectX 12

Players and weapons

  • Original TC:E soldier models with animations and ground shadows.

Original HUD

  • The body silhouette changes colour as you take damage.

Multiplayer
