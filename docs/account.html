Optional account

Your progress, on every PC

An account is never required to play. Sign in with Discord to keep your rank and stats when you reinstall or switch PCs, claim your name on every official server and get a public profile.

Progress follows you

Link each PC you play on. Rank, rating and every stat add up to one record, and a new PC or a lost key never resets it.

Your name is yours

Official servers keep your name for you, look-alikes included. Nobody else plays under it.

A profile worth sharing

Match history, best weapons and records at one short address, with your Discord shown only if you want it.

Sign in with DiscordNo email, no password

We ask Discord who you are and nothing else. Read what we keep on the privacy page.

Pick your name

3 to 20 characters. Pick your colour in the launcher; the name itself is plain.

Privacy

Your stats count either way. A hidden account keeps its name reserved. The weekly report is a private message from our bot with your week in numbers.

Looking for a squad

Let others know you want to team up. You show on the servers page and in our Discord until the time runs out.

Passkeys

A passkey (Windows Hello, Face ID, your phone or a security key) is a second way in, so you keep your account even if you lose your Discord.

    Linked PCs

    Every PC you link adds to one record. Lost a PC or reinstalled? Link the new one and everything comes back.

      On another PC? Open the launcher there, choose Link with phone and scan the code while you are signed in here.

      Your data

      Download everything we keep about your account, or delete it. Deleting frees your name and unlinks every PC. Your match stats stay anonymous on the leaderboards, as they are for players without an account.

      Download my data

      Lost or stolen PC

      Unlinks every PC and signs you out everywhere at once. Your rank and stats stay with the account: link the PCs you still have again afterwards.

      Accounts open with the next update. Until then, everyone plays exactly as today.
