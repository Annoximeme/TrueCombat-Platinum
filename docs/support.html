Support

Keep the servers running

TrueCombat: Platinum is free and always will be. If you enjoy it and want to help keep it online, you can chip in. It is entirely optional and never expected.

Support us on Ko-fi Join the Discord
Our promise

Free means free

Everyone plays on equal terms. Supporting the project changes nothing about how the game plays.

  1. 01

    No store

    There is nothing to buy in the game. No skins for sale, no loot boxes, no premium currency.

  2. 02

    No battle pass

    Every weapon, map and mode is unlocked from the first match, for every player.

  3. 03

    No advantages

    A contribution never buys a better gun, a faster rank or a place in the queue.

  4. 04

    No pressure

    No pop-ups, no reminders in the menus, no countdowns. Supporting is always your choice.

Where it goes

Straight into the infrastructure

The game is free, but running it is not. Every contribution goes towards the costs of keeping TrueCombat: Platinum online: the official servers, the master server behind the in-game browser, this website and the live leaderboards.

Anything beyond our monthly costs is set aside for what comes next, such as extra servers in more regions as the community grows.

How to help

Every bit counts

  1. 01

    One-off

    Leave a contribution of any size on Ko-fi, by card or PayPal. No account needed.

  2. 02

    Monthly

    Become a Supporter for a small monthly amount and help cover the servers every month. Cancel whenever you like.

  3. 03

    A thank-you

    Supporters receive the Supporter role on our Discord. It is a thank-you, nothing more, and it has no effect in the game.

  4. 04

    Without spending

    Play, bring your friends, host a server, or report bugs on the Discord. That helps just as much.

Contributions are voluntary gifts to the project. They are not tax-deductible and are not refundable. Payments are handled securely by Ko-fi; we never see your card details.
