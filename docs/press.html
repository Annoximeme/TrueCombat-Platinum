Fact sheet

At a glance

A native 64-bit Windows rebuild of TrueCombat: Elite, the realistic modern-combat total conversion for Enemy Territory. DirectX 12 rendering, the original maps, soldiers and HUD, and a launcher that hosts servers and installs signed updates.
