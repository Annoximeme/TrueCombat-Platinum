Every fight on the record

Player profiles are about to get a lot deeper, and unranked servers count too. Here is what we track, and the rules that keep every number honest.

In this post
  1. What you get
  2. The rules
  3. A few honest limits

Until now, your profile only filled up on ranked servers, and it told you the basics: kills, deaths, headshots, wins. With the next update that changes. Every verified match counts, ranked or unranked, and your profile becomes a full service record.

What you get

Your profile breaks your game down the way a coach would:

  • Combat. K/D, damage per round, headshot rate, accuracy, assists, kills per minute, longest and average kill distance, best streak.
  • The moments that decide rounds. Opening kills, clutches won and your best clutch, trade kills, revenge kills, double, triple and quad kills, round MVPs.
  • Where your hits land. Head, body and legs, as a share of every hit on another player.
  • Playstyle. Aimed against hip-fire kills, standing, crouching and prone, leaning and airborne kills, knife and grenade kills.
  • Objectives. Bomb plants and defuses, flag captures and returns, rounds won.
  • Maps and modes. Win rate, K/D and time played on each.
  • Rivals. Your nemesis, your favourite target, and a head-to-head view against any player you search for.
  • Activity. A year of matches at a glance.

The leaderboards get a new Stat leaders board next to the main ranking. Pick any of these stats, then narrow it to ranked matches only, a map, a mode, a weapon or the last 7 or 30 days. Ratios such as K/D and accuracy only list players with a fair sample, so three lucky rounds don't top the board.

The rules

More stats only matter if you can trust them. A match only counts when all of this holds:

  1. Verified server. The report is signed with a key we issued to that server. That covers our official servers and community servers we have approved.
  2. Anti-cheat on from start to finish. The server records its Easy Anti-Cheat state when the match starts, when it ends, and every second in between. If it was off at any point, the whole match is left out.
  3. Real players only. A kill, a hit or a point of damage counts only when both sides are human. Bots can fill the server, but nothing you do to a bot, or a bot does to you, lands on your record.
  4. A real match. At least two real players on opposite sides, each in the game for at least two minutes. Warm-up, practice matches, password-protected servers and anything with cheats switched on never count.
  5. No farming. Two players on the same connection never count against each other. Only ten kills on the same opponent count per match, and pairs who mostly play only each other are flagged for review.
  6. No idle time. If you stand still for more than a minute, that time is not counted as time played.
  7. A second look. Numbers no fair player reaches, such as near-perfect accuracy over a long match, go to a review list that only the team sees. We can remove a single match or a player's whole record.

Your skill rating still only moves on ranked servers. Unranked matches still earn experience toward your rank, and every other stat.

A few honest limits

On a server with bots, a missed shot can't be tied to who it was aimed at. So accuracy only comes from matches without bots. Headshot rate and hit locations work everywhere, because they count actual hits on real players.

Detailed stats start with the update that switches anti-cheat on for our servers. Matches played before it stay on your profile, but they only carry the totals we recorded at the time.

As before, there is no account to create. The game recognises you by a key it creates on your PC, and only a scrambled ID is ever published.
