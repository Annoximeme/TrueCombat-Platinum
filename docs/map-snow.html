Game modes

Playing Snow

  • Bomb. Terrorists plant at site A (Main Storage) or site B (Southern Depot), and SpecOps defend or defuse. Measured in a straight line, site B is nearer the SpecOps spawn and site A nearer the Terrorist spawn.
  • Flag Capture. Each team guards a backpack near its own spawn. Steal the other one and carry it home. First team to three wins.
  • Elimination and Team Elimination. One life per round. With 36 spawn points a side, a full server still starts spread out.
  • Team Deathmatch. Respawns on, first team to 20 kills.
