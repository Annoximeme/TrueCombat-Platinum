v0.38.1Latest# TrueCombat: Platinum 0.38.1#65 · 0.38.1-sandbox35 · 17 Nov 2026

Hotfix

  • Fixed some antivirus programs (Avast, AVG, Avira) wrongly flagging the game during the 0.38.0 update, which stopped the game from starting.
  • Fixed the weapon and arms swinging wildly while moving.

Launcher update, 18 november 2026

  • When an update fails, the launcher now shows the exact reason in Settings > Updates, in the status panel and on the PLAY button, instead of a generic "Update check failed".
  • If your antivirus blocks or quarantines a game file, the launcher names the file and asks you to add the TrueCombat Platinum folder as an exception.
  • Missing or damaged game files are detected at startup and replaced automatically. Verify and repair now works in every situation, including after a failed update.
  • Leftovers from an interrupted or blocked update are cleaned up automatically, and downloads retry when the connection drops.
  • Fixed PLAY repeatedly offering INSTALL when the game engine was missing.
  • Every update step is now logged to updates\launcher-update.log in the game folder.

v0.38.0Our biggest update yet. Content from TrueCombat: CQB arrives on an experimental front, you can now take weapons off the battlefield, there's a firing range, and a long list of changes makes every firefight hit harder.#64 · 0.38.0-sandbox34 · 17 Nov 2026

Experimental: truecombat: cqb comes to platinum

  • Harbour. A new close-quarters map, fully rebuilt for DX12 with its own minimap and tactical map.
  • Experimental Arsenal. Thirteen weapons enter testing, including the M1911, USP .40, Shorty, MP9, Mini Uzi, SCAR, SG 552, REC7, G3 and the M110 marksman rifle, with dual M1911s and dual Shorties. Find them under the new Experimental tab in the Deployment Menu.
  • CQB Rules. A fast-paced playlist with Bodycount (first team to 20 kills, quick respawns), perks such as Lightweight, Protected, Tank and Commando, and health that recovers after a few seconds out of the fight.
  • New movement. Commando can grab ledges and climb, corner sliding keeps you moving along walls, and spawns place you away from the enemy.
  • New optics and sound. ACOG, EOTech, Leupold and M68 sights, a new radio voice and indoor reverb.

New features

  • Battlefield pickups. Weapons now drop when a player falls. Walk up to an enemy's weapon and press Use to swap it for your own, with whatever ammunition its owner left behind. Enabled on official Ranked and Unranked servers.
  • Firing range. Choose Firing range from the Play menu to sight in every weapon on targets from 10 to 100 metres. Each hit shows where it landed, the damage and the distance, with your accuracy for the session.
  • Vaulting. Press jump at a low wall, crate or window to vault over it or climb on top. The classic mantle stays as it was.
  • Know when to jump in. With the launcher in the background, Windows lets you know when people are playing on a public server. One click and you're in. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Female soldier option. Choose Male or Female under Options > Customise Game > Player. Both use the same hitboxes, so the choice is purely visual.

Aim and performance

  • Aiming down sights now zooms and changes sensitivity the instant you press the key, with no round-trip delay.
  • New Aim speed option: keep the Classic feel, or match your aim sensitivity to your hip-fire feel.
  • New Frame Rate Limit option in Options > System, from 60 to 500 FPS. The launcher no longer forces 125 FPS.
  • Movement, stamina, recoil recovery and jumping now behave the same at every frame rate.
  • Lower input latency on DX12, with tighter frame pacing.

Firefights

  • Explosions now shake the camera by distance, throw dust from the ceiling, scorch the ground and leave dirt on your screen.
  • Indoor haze, drifting dust, falling leaves and birds that scatter at the first shot bring every map to life.
  • Impacts react to what they hit: sparks on metal, splinters on wood, glass that breaks properly, and water splashes.
  • Blood spreads into pools, uniforms pick up blood and dirt, and players flinch when hit and fall according to where they were hit.
  • The death camera now drops and rolls with the body before fading out.
  • A replay of the final kill closes out each round.
  • New first-person touches: a dip when you land, the gun pulls back at walls, and spent magazines drop on reload.
  • Footprints in the snow, muzzle flashes that cast shadows, heat shimmer from hot barrels and gunfire that sounds different indoors and at range.
  • Combat voice lines: soldiers call out contacts, kills and casualties.
  • An optional Auto-hide HUD fades the HUD out when you're not in the fight. It is off by default.

HUD

  • Minimap. Choose a round (new default) or square minimap in Options.
  • Bomb mode. Everyone sees "Bomb planted at &lt;location&gt;" when the bomb goes down. A new bomb card shows the site, a countdown and defuse progress, and the carrier gets clear hints, including a warning when they try to plant outside a site.
  • Hit and suppression effects now fade smoothly at the screen edges instead of showing dark bands.
  • Kill messages show long weapon names in full, for example the AK-74M Spetsnaz.
  • Grenades now appear by their proper names: M67, M84 and M83.
  • The game window is now titled TrueCombat: Platinum.

Weapons and visuals

  • Upgraded first-person weapon models and materials, with sharper metal, wear and texture detail on DX12.
  • New player gear for both teams.

Bots

  • Bots vary their routes in Bomb and Elimination, so rounds no longer play out the same way twice.
  • When a player drops out mid-match, a bot holds their place until they return.

Launcher and installer

  • New Play page artwork captured in-game.
  • Optional crash and hardware reports help us fix problems faster. The launcher asks once, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy.
  • A much smaller installer: setup is now under 1 MB and fetches the latest verified launcher during installation.
  • Every download on truecombat.eu is now scanned by VirusTotal and listed with its SHA-256 checksum.

Fixes and stability

  • Cleaner map loading, with stray console errors removed.
  • We've tightened and expanded our security measures.

Audio

  • Fixed game audio going silent until a restart when Windows switched or reset your audio device. Sound now recovers by itself within a few seconds.

HUD

  • Very dark name colours are brightened just enough to stay readable on the HUD, while keeping their hue.
  • The in-game What's New card is updated for this release.

DirectX 12

  • Steadier skies with DLSS, DLAA and TAA. Cloud layers, the horizon and the sun no longer shimmer while you stand still or turn.

Performance

v0.36.00.36.0 adds voice chat, team pings, smarter bots, a full audio overhaul and a name colour picker. It sharpens online shooting, keeps the install folder from growing and sends F12 bug reports straight to the development team. Update through the launcher, or install fresh with TrueCombat-Platinum-Setup.exe.#58 · 0.36.0-sandbox28 · 13 Nov 2026

New: voice chat

  • Team radio: hold H to talk to your team. Enemies never hear it.
  • Local voice: hold N to speak out loud. Everyone nearby hears you, enemies included.
  • Options > Voice: voice on/off, volume, microphone gain and input device, push to talk or voice activation (with an adjustable level), radio effect, mute the enemy team, and an on-screen list of who is talking.
  • Microphone test: hear yourself without sending anything.
  • Mute players: mute single players from the mute menu.
  • Keys: both voice keys can be rebound in game and in the launcher's Controls. B stays the radio menu.
  • Voice works on servers that enable it.

New: team pings

  • Press middle mouse to mark a position, or point at an enemy to call them out. Your team sees a marker for 5 seconds with a short notice.
  • Team modes only. Pings are rate-limited to keep the screen clean, and cg_pings 0 hides other players' marks.

New: bots

  • Bots now read the map. They spread across multiple routes, take doorways one at a time, slow down and check corners, and aim down sights when holding an angle.
  • Bots take a moment to spot you. Distance, lighting, movement and stance all count, so instant snap reactions are gone.
  • Bots no longer camp spawn exits.
  • Personalities: each bot now has a name and a personality from a 32-strong roster. Some play aggressively, others play it safe, and each has its own preferred weapons.
  • Callouts: bots call out enemy positions by map location on team chat and report bomb and flag events (adjust with bot_chat).
  • Team orders: bots respond to typed team orders such as "follow me", "hold here" and "push", and always confirm they are bots when asked.
  • Adaptive difficulty: with humans in the game, bots adjust their skill to keep matches close (disable with bot_adaptive 0).

New: audio overhaul

  • More sounds at once: the game now plays twice as many sounds at the same time. In a full firefight, enemy footsteps and gunfire take priority over ambience and shell casings instead of being dropped.
  • Balanced mix: every sound in the game has been levelled for loudness, category by category, and a master limiter stops loud moments from clipping.
  • Gunfire takes the stage: nearby shots and explosions briefly lower the rest of the mix so they cut through.
  • Natural variation: footsteps, gunfire, impacts and casings vary slightly in pitch, so repeated sounds no longer sound identical.
  • Bullet crack and whizz: rounds passing close to you crack and whizz by.
  • Explosion aftermath: a nearby blast muffles your hearing for a moment and lands with a low thump. The ringing in your ears is now a softer tone (the original beep is still available).
  • Options > Audio: a new page for the new sound settings, including the bullet crack, the ringing tone and how strongly gunfire lowers the rest of the mix.

New: name colours

  • Colour each letter of your name in the launcher: Settings > Controls > Player > Name colours. It includes a live preview, click-and-drag selection and 26 colours.
  • Coloured names now show correctly on the scoreboard, in chat and in the Deployment Menu with every ET colour, not just the first ten.

Online play and hit registration

  • Smoother updates: the launcher now asks servers for 64 updates per second instead of 40, so movement and hits update more smoothly on 128-tick servers.
  • Hit marks match the server: your game now uses the server's exact rules to decide whether a shot hit, so hit marks and the server agree.
  • Heads and legs register: shots at heads and legs that stick out past a player's hitbox now count. Before, these sometimes missed at certain angles and stances.
  • Steadier on poor connections: your input is resent until the server confirms it, and packet duplication adapts on its own to unstable connections.
  • 128 updates per second: a new Options > System > Network updates setting lets you receive 128 updates per second on 128-tick servers. 64 stays the default and uses about half the bandwidth.
  • Steadier at high update rates: the game now keeps a longer history of server updates, so packet loss and lag spikes no longer force full resyncs at 128 updates per second.
  • Fairer for low-ping players: lag compensation now rewinds at most 250 ms (was 400 ms).
  • New net graph: cg_netGraph 3 shows ping, packet loss in both directions, updates per second, snapshot gaps, prediction corrections and packet duplication.

Storage

  • The install folder no longer grows: the DX12 shader cache used to save a fresh copy on every map change, which could add up to several gigabytes. It now keeps a single copy that is reused across servers and maps.
  • Size limits: the launcher keeps caches, replays, screenshots and bug reports under a size limit, removing the oldest files first. Settings > Storage shows how much space replays and caches use.
  • The first start of launcher 2.1.0 clears any oversized cache left by earlier versions.
  • Updates no longer clear the shader cache twice, so your first match after an update loads faster.

Bug reports (f12)

  • F12 reports now go straight to the development team: description, map, position, server, match, build, renderer, ping, frame rate, recent console lines and a screenshot. Hardware info is optional.
  • Every report is still saved on your PC. If sending fails, the game tells you and keeps the report.
  • cg_bugReportUpload 0 keeps reports on your PC only.
  • bugreport <text> in the console files a report straight away.

Security

  • We've tightened and expanded our security measures.

Launcher 2.1.0

  • Player count: the Play button shows how many people are playing and your ping ("12 playing · 38 ms").
  • New options: name colour picker and voice keys in Controls, in English, German, French and Polish.

Weapons and visuals

  • M4 SOPMOD red dot: the M68 sight now shows a crisp red dot when aimed. The reticle no longer shows as a blurry square.
  • DLSS: if a DLSS override in the NVIDIA App takes over, the game now says so instead of failing silently.

Fixes

  • Hit markers on edge shots (head and leg grazes) now always match what the server counts.
  • Options > System > Calibrate Brightness opens correctly again.
  • Your own predicted shots now play the correct sound for the weapon you are holding.
  • Spectators stay spectators after a map change or restart instead of being put into a team.
  • Footsteps and view bob now follow toggled walk and sprint correctly (cg_toggleWalk / cg_toggleSprint).
  • Server browser: after "Invalid password", Reconnect now reconnects to that server. Joining from the in-game browser also works mid-match.
  • Local voice and quick voice chat (V menu) now work in Practice and free-for-all modes.
  • F8 replays: clips need at least 5 seconds of play, and the "Clip saved" notice shows the real clip length.

Behind the scenes

  • Every round is now recorded (winner, kills, objectives) for match history and leaderboards. No personal data is stored.
  • Opus voice codec added, with its licence included under Licences.

v0.35.0Easy Anti-Cheat, a rebuilt launcher, an in-game server browser, a proper warm-up, grenade cooking and a long list of movement, weapon and map fixes from the latest playtest. Update through the launcher, or install fresh with TrueCombat-Platinum-Setup.exe.#57 · 0.35.0-sandbox27 · 12 Nov 2026

Easy anti-cheat

  • TrueCombat: Platinum now ships with Easy Anti-Cheat. PLAY starts the game through the Easy Anti-Cheat bootstrapper, and the installer sets up the anti-cheat service once (Windows asks for permission).
  • Servers that require anti-cheat show a green shield in the in-game server list and an EASY ANTI-CHEAT tag in the launcher; servers with anti-cheat switched on but not required show ANTI-CHEAT ON. The connect screen states the server's anti-cheat status.
  • Anti-cheat sign-in uses a TrueCombat identity created on your PC. No account or sign-up is needed.

Launcher 2.0

  • Redesigned launcher with English, German, French and Polish text.
  • Server cards show the game mode, verified community servers and when the list was last checked.
  • truecombat:// join links open the game and connect straight to the server. A second copy of the game no longer takes these links over from your installed copy.
  • A name set in the game, including colour codes, is no longer replaced by the launcher on the next start. A name typed in the launcher still takes priority. Names can be up to 35 characters.
  • The Controls page now includes the Walk key (Caps Lock by default).

Server browser and warm-up

  • FIND SERVERS in the main menu and the Esc menu opens the in-game server browser: browse, filter and join servers without returning to the launcher.
  • Warm-up is now a practice phase before the first round: you spawn straight away, respawn one second after dying, and can try weapons, change your loadout and talk to other players. The match starts after a 30-second countdown once two players are present (server hosts: g_warmup). Warm-up kills and deaths do not count.

Movement

  • Jumping onto low objects works as in the original game: standing next to an object such as a dumpster, the jump is no longer reduced, so you can climb on it. Higher ledges are still climbed with the ledge climb.
  • Aiming down sights now slows you down as in the original game.
  • Footsteps and head bob follow the original timing. Crouched footsteps are slower and quieter, and walking, aiming and leaning are quiet.
  • You can no longer fire while sprinting. The weapon is ready again a quarter of a second after you stop.

Gameplay
