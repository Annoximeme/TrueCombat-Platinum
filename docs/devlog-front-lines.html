All devlog posts Update 0.39.0

UpdateNext update · in testing18 Nov 20264 min readTeam Terminus

Front lines

Bots that listen and plan, four classic community maps, a rebuilt launcher with friends, and a new layer of light, weather and sound across every map.

In this post
  1. Opponents that think
  2. Four classics return
  3. Light, weather and water
  4. Closer to the soldier
  5. Launcher 3.0
  6. Also in this update

0.39.0 is about the moments between shots: the sound of someone moving on the other side of a wall, the sun breaking through a doorway on Snow, a team that regroups before it retakes a site. It is also our largest launcher update since Platinum began.

Opponents that think

Bots in Platinum have always been able to shoot. Now they listen. Footsteps and gunfire give your position away, and a bot that loses sight of you remembers where you were and plays around it.

Bomb rounds play like a match. Attackers agree on a plan before the round starts, can cover their way in with smoke, and spread out around the charge once it is down. Defenders hold their sites, fall back when they are outnumbered and gather to retake together instead of trickling in one by one. Over a session, each team learns which plans work on which map.

![Bots pushing onto a bomb site on Snow](img/devlog/front-lines-bots-snow-push.webp "Attackers move onto a site together on Snow.") ![Defenders moving in to retake a site on Snow](img/devlog/front-lines-bots-snow-retake.webp "Defenders gather and move in to retake the site.")

We also tracked down the short hitches some of you noticed at the end of a round on servers with bots. They are gone.

Four classics return

Villa 2.1, Shozen, Snowtown and Chlite were among the most played community maps in TrueCombat:Elite. All four now ship with Platinum and play on Unranked servers. We left the maps exactly as their authors made them and cleaned up a handful of sounds that had not aged well.

Stadtrand gets something back too: its second bomb site works again, on every server.

![Villa 2.1](img/devlog/front-lines-villa.webp "Villa 2.1 in the evening, Ultra preset, DirectX 12.") ![Snowtown](img/devlog/front-lines-snowtown.webp "Snowtown, Ultra preset, DirectX 12.") ![Shozen](img/devlog/front-lines-shozen.webp "Shozen, Ultra preset, DirectX 12.") ![Chlite](img/devlog/front-lines-chlite.webp "Chlite, Ultra preset, DirectX 12.") ![Stadtrand with both bomb sites on the HUD](img/devlog/front-lines-stadtrand-bomb-hud.webp "Stadtrand: sites A and B both on the HUD again.")

Light, weather and water

Baked lighting is smoother, with soft shadow edges on walls and floors. Trees, grass and canvas move in the wind. On DirectX 12, sunlight now breaks through gaps in the scenery as soft shafts, wet ground picks up reflections, and brick and cobblestone gain real depth up close. Water ripples when players wade through it or rounds hit it, fire and explosions are far more detailed, and smoke lights up briefly with every blast.

On Snow, frost now gathers at the edges of your view outdoors and melts when you step inside.

Ranked keeps a level field: sun shafts, glare, colour mood, frost and lit smoke stay off there, so everyone sees the same picture.

Closer to the soldier

Your gear now makes noise as you move, a magazine rattles when you sprint, and the last round ends with a dry click. Drop low on health and a slow heartbeat sets in, quickening as you near death, while the world dulls and loses colour. After a long sprint you catch your breath.

What you carry is now on your body for everyone to see: sidearm on the right thigh, knife on the left hip, grenades on the belt. The support hand finally sits on the handguard of every rifle in third person, and fallen soldiers come to rest naturally against walls, slopes and stairs.

For riflemen, a new focus zoom: while aiming, hold the right mouse button to steady the view with a slight extra zoom.

Launcher 3.0

The launcher has been rebuilt from the ground up. The Play page shows live servers with their map and players, the server browser gains favourites, recent servers and search, and news opens as full articles. Friends lets you add each other by code, see who is playing and join in one click. It is optional and stays off until you turn it on. Downloads can be paused and resumed, and new accessibility settings cover high contrast, larger text, colour-blind friendly markers and reduced motion. The launcher is now also available in Dutch.

Also in this update

  • Northport's harbour ambience plays again. It had been silent since the original release.
  • Players no longer slide down slopes while standing still.
  • Pulling the pin on a grenade is now audible.
  • When spectating, the player you follow now sprints, aims and sways as they do in their own view.

Full patch notes are on the release page. See you on the front line.
