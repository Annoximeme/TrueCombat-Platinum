Location names

Location names on Village

Game modes

Playing Village

  • Bomb. Terrorists plant at site A or site B, and SpecOps defend or defuse. Measured in a straight line, both sites are nearer the SpecOps spawn: site B is the close one and site A the far one.
  • Flag Capture. Each team guards a backpack near its own spawn. Steal the other one and carry it home. First team to three wins.
  • Elimination and Team Elimination. One life per round. With 30 spawn points a side, a full server still starts spread out.
  • Team Deathmatch. Respawns on, first team to 20 kills.
