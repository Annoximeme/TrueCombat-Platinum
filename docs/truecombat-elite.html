TrueCombat: Elite

TrueCombat: Elite, playable today

TC:E was one of the most demanding tactical shooters of its time. TrueCombat: Platinum brings it to modern Windows as a free 64-bit game, with the maps, weapons and movement you remember.

01

What TrueCombat: Elite was

TrueCombat: Elite, TC:E for short, is a free total conversion for Wolfenstein: Enemy Territory, made by Team Terminator. It followed the original TrueCombat for Quake III Arena and swapped the engine's Second World War for modern combat.

It played like nothing else on that engine. One life per round. Iron sights and real weapon handling. Stamina that punished careless sprinting, leaning around corners, and maps built for slow, deliberate play. A single mistake ended your round, and that is exactly why people stayed for years.

02

Why TC:E is hard to play today

The original still runs, but it shows its age. It is a 32-bit mod on an engine from 2003, it needs a separate Enemy Territory install, and it has no installer or updater of its own.

The bigger problem is finding a match. Most of the public servers that carried the community have gone quiet, and a returning player has no obvious place to start.

03

What TrueCombat: Platinum is

Platinum rebuilds TC:E as a native 64-bit Windows game with a DirectX 12 renderer. The aim is simple: everything that was in TC:E should look, sound and handle the way you remember it.

  • The original maps, soldiers and HUD. The HUD is matched against the original pixel by pixel.
  • The original movement. Sprint, run, crouch and prone speeds come straight from the TC:E values, with leaning, ladders and swimming.
  • The full arsenal. All 27 guns, the knife and every grenade, with the original damage, recoil and spread.
  • All seven game modes, from Elimination to Bomb and Flag Capture.

Coming back after a few years

If you played TC:E, you already know how to play Platinum. The keys, the pace and the maps are where you left them.

  1. Download the installer and let the launcher set up the game.
  2. Join Official #2 from the Servers tab to shake off the rust, then move to Official #1 when you want your matches to count.
  3. Say hello on the Discord. Veterans and new players set up their matches there.
