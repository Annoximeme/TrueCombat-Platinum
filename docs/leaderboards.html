How it works

How rankings work

RankEarned through experience: kills, headshots, round wins, objectives, match wins and time in the field. 21 ranks, from Recruit to General. Team kills cost experience.
Skill ratingEveryone starts at 1000. Beat a stronger side and you gain more; lose to a weaker one and you drop more. Your share depends on how long you played.
Combat recordKills, deaths and headshots against the enemy. Team kills and suicides are tracked separately.
WinsMatches your team won. Your profile also lists your weapons, accuracy and last 20 matches.

Only ranked servers report matches, and every report is signed with that server's own key. Practice matches, matches with cheats enabled and bots never count. The game recognises you by a key it creates on your PC the first time it starts, so there is nothing to sign up for. Only a scrambled ID is published, never that key or your IP address. Keep your etkey file when you reinstall and your rank comes with you.
