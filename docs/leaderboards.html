How it works

How rankings work

RankEarned through experience: kills, headshots, round wins, objectives, match wins and time in the field. 21 ranks, from Recruit to General. Team kills cost experience.
Skill ratingEveryone starts at 1000. Beat a stronger side and you gain more; lose to a weaker one and you drop more. Your share depends on how long you played.
Combat recordOnly fights against real players count, even when bots share the server. Team kills and suicides are tracked separately.
WinsMatches your team won. Your profile breaks down every weapon, map, rival and clutch.

Matches count from official and approved servers, ranked and unranked, with Easy Anti-Cheat on from the first second to the last. Every report is signed with that server's own key. Kills on bots, warm-up, idle time, practice matches and matches with cheats enabled never count, and the skill rating only moves on ranked servers. The game recognises you by a key it creates on your PC the first time it starts, so there is nothing to sign up for. Only a scrambled ID is published, never that key or your IP address. Keep your etkey file when you reinstall and your rank comes with you.
