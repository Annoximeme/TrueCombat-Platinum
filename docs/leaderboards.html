How rankings work

Kills and deathsEvery frag on a listed server. Team kills are not counted.
HeadshotsShown as a share of your kills.
WinsRounds your team won while you were on it.
Top weaponThe gun you got the most kills with.

Only servers that opt in send match reports, and practice matches are never counted. Players are told apart by a scrambled ID, never by their IP address.
